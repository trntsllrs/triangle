HOW TO PLAY

  • Tap a shape to pick it up, then tap the grid to drop it. Or drag it straight across.
  • Fill a whole row, column or 3×3 box to shatter it.
  • Clear more than one at once for a combo multiplier.
  • The round ends when none of your three shapes fit.
  • Hard mode only shatters full rows and columns — 3×3 boxes stay put. Scores are ranked on their own board.
